From zero to playing

Three steps, about twenty seconds
Mile 1

One person opens

Whoever's holding a phone starts a room. Nothing to configure, nothing to name.

Mile 2

Everyone taps join

Nearby devices see the room instantly over Bluetooth. Up to eight seats, first tap first served.

Scores tally as you play. Back on WiFi later, results sync to the global leaderboards.
