One room, one app

Eight people, eight screens, no cables.

Everyone joins from their own iPhone or iPad. Seats fill in tap order, and anyone who doesn't get one watches the round live as a spectator.

Peer to peer

The devices talk to each other, not to a server.

Bluetooth and local WiFi carry the whole game. Play in the car, on a plane, in a basement, at a cabin with one bar and no patience for it.

Nothing to sign up for

Open it and you're playing.

No email, no password, no profile. Sign in with Apple is optional — take it if you want your name to stick to your scores on the global leaderboards, which sync whenever you're next online.
