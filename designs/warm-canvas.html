What you get

A game night that fits in a pocket.

One download covers the whole shelf — and it works in places where nothing else does.

37

Games, one app

Glyph, Crossword, Sudoku, Geode, Crossplay and Triangle Gather, plus trivia, drawing, word, storytelling and hidden-role games.

8

Devices per room

Everyone plays on their own iPhone or iPad. Individual games keep their own smaller limits where it matters.

0

Internet required

Devices connect peer-to-peer over Bluetooth and local WiFi. Basements, planes, cabins, tunnels — it doesn't care.

0

Accounts to create

Open and play. Sign in with Apple is optional and only links this device — it's never a wall in front of a game.

What the app keeps

Every round leaves a record.

Results are stored on the host device as you play and uploaded once, idempotently, when connectivity returns. Nothing is lost because you were out of range.
