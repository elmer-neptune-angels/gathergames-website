GatherGames landing page concepts

Four directions for gathergames.io, each a complete page with a prominent App Store button in a pre-launch state. Same content throughout — the difference is the point of view.

Concept 01
Back Seat

Highway signage. Leads with the road trip — green sign panels, a lane line running between sections, mile markers for the setup steps.
Concept 02
Eight Seats

Lamplight over a table at night. The hero animates the actual mechanic — eight seats filling in tap order. The premium app-marketing option.
Concept 03
No Signal

Apple-product-page restraint. One idea carries the page: turn the internet off, keep playing. Accent is iOS airplane-mode orange, used three times.
Concept 04
Deck of 37

Risograph print and game-box tactility. The collection is the hero — a fanned hand up top, all 37 cards laid out below.

Eight Seats — leaderboard variants

Three ways to work leaderboards and stats into concept 02, in the same visual language. They differ in how much weight the leaderboard carries.

02 · A
Podium

Seat-ring hero stays. A full global leaderboard becomes the third beat of the page, with your row called out and three stat tiles beside it.
02 · B
Top Table

The leaderboard takes the hero and the seat ring moves down to how-it-works. Most competitive read: somebody has to be first.
02 · C
Career

Personal angle. Four stat tiles, your best score per game, and the global board as context rather than the headline.
Concepts only — the live site still runs from /index.html. Each page carries an AT LAUNCH comment marking the App Store link to fill in. Leaderboard rows and stats are sample data.
