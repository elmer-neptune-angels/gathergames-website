About GatherGames

GatherGames is a local-multiplayer game collection for iPhone and iPad. Everyone plays on their own device, connecting peer-to-peer over Bluetooth and local WiFi — so gameplay needs no account and no internet connection.

From road trip classics to word, trivia, drawing, and hidden-role games, up to eight players can jump in instantly. When you're back online, results can sync to global leaderboards.

One Room, Everyone In

37 Games

Road Trip Bingo, License Plate Search, Glyph, Crossword, Sudoku, trivia, drawing, word, storytelling, and hidden-role games.

Up to 8 Players

Everyone joins from their own iPhone or iPad. Seats fill in tap order — anyone not playing watches live as a spectator.

No Internet Required

Devices connect peer-to-peer over Bluetooth and local WiFi. Play in the car, on a plane, at the cabin — anywhere.

No Accounts

No sign-ups, no logins — open the app and play. Optional Sign in with Apple for a stable player identity.
